# Authors and maintainers

## PlayStation 3 adaptation

**DeepDarkDaddy — PS3 port project lead and maintainer (2026).**

Credited for leading and testing the PS3 adaptation of the Genesis Plus GX/PUNCHiUM core, coordinating compatibility debugging, validating and integrating the big-endian sprite/animation correction (STEP1495L) and PCM sound-effect/voice correction (STEP1495X), and preparing this verified source release.

The PS3-specific improvements were completed through collaborative investigation and testing; the original emulator and PUNCHiUM implementation remain the work of their upstream developers. This attribution is for the PS3 port and its compatibility work, **not** a claim to authorship of the Paprium game, original Genesis Plus GX emulator, or original PUNCHiUM extensions.
